ENDORSEMENT REQUEST - what to do
================================

STEP 1
On the arXiv page, click "request endorsement".
arXiv gives you a SIX-CHARACTER CODE. Copy it.

STEP 2
Find someone who can endorse for cs.LG. arXiv's rule is that they
need at least 3 papers in that archive within a five-year window,
so in practice: any professor who has recent arXiv papers in
cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL or cs.CV.

How to check before you email them:
  - google "<professor name> arxiv"
  - open their arXiv author page
  - look for cs.LG or cs.AI in the subject line of recent papers

Ask, in this order:
  1. A professor whose course you have taken
  2. Your academic advisor
  3. Any ECE or Khoury professor doing machine learning

STEP 3
Send the email below. Replace [CODE] and [NAME].

NOTES
-----
- Endorsing is not co-authorship, not a review, and carries no
  liability. Say so in the email; many people do not know this.
- The submission stays saved on arXiv while you wait. Nothing is
  lost. Find it again at https://arxiv.org/user
- Ask two or three people at once. It costs nothing and one may
  simply be away.
